About the three styles

  • A · One-tap: smallest. Each tap moves Au → Ag → Pt → Au. You can't see the other metals until you tap.
  • B · 3-way switch (recommended): all three always visible, one tap to any metal, sliding colour thumb like the Rates toggle.
  • C · Chip + menu: compact like A but shows names and purity ranges. Easy to add Palladium later; needs two taps.

Demo only: values you type are kept per metal on this device. Live prices are best-effort; if a live price can't be fetched, type it in manually.
